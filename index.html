Academic Profile

Nonlinear PDEs & spatial epidemic modeling

Professor specializing in nonlinear partial differential equations and spatial epidemic modeling. My research focuses on reaction–diffusion systems with nonlocal dispersal, age-structured models, and delay-differential equations, with applications in epidemiology and ecological dynamics.
